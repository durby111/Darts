Game Options
Points subtracted from the next leg's starting score when you lose a leg. Floor is always 121.
Standard 9 innings: each player throws 3 darts per inning at the inning's number (1–9). Single = 1 run, Double = 2 runs, Triple = 3 runs. Highest total runs after 9 innings wins.
18 holes (1 → 18). Each player throws 3 darts per hole at the hole's number. Triple = 1 stroke (eagle), Double = 2 (birdie), Single = 3 (par), miss = 5. Lowest total strokes wins.
Standard: rounds 1→7, 3 darts per round at the round's number. Score = face × multiplier. Single + Double + Triple in one turn = SHANGHAI — instant win. Otherwise highest total after round 7 wins.
🎲 Chaos Cricket: 6 numbers are drawn at random (plus Bull) when the game starts — every match plays a different board. Standard cricket marks, closing and points rules apply. "Play Again" re-rolls the numbers.
Halve-It: 13 targets (12, 13, 14, 15, Double, 16, 17, 18, Triple, 19, 20, 25, Bullseye). Each turn = 3 darts; score = sum × multiplier. If you miss with all 3 darts on ANY target, your score halves. Most competitive version. Highest score at the end wins.
Both teammates must close a target before the team can score on it. Team lead is capped at 400 points.
