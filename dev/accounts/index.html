Your profile

Names are labels, not account identifiers. Guests have no account ID and are excluded from lifetime statistics. The existing scorer’s anonymous sign-in is separate and unchanged.

Your recorded matches

Includes organizer-recorded tournaments and scorekeeper-recorded casual matches, clearly labeled by source. These are not independently certified scores. Only matches linked to your verified player ID count here; guests are excluded. Minnesota and other Cricket variants use marks per round (MPR).

Firebase setup and DEV data

This is a separate DEV account system in the existing BlakeOut Firebase project. No third-party analytics is used. Branded verification and password-reset requests are processed through Cloudflare, Firebase, and Resend. Resend receives your email address and the account-action email for delivery; your password is not sent to Cloudflare or Resend.

Project administrators must enable Authentication → Sign-in method → Email/Password, authorize the hosting domain, and publish the isolated blakeoutDevProfiles, blakeoutDevTournaments, blakeoutDevRosterPrivate, and blakeoutDevResults rules from firestore.rules. Email link (passwordless sign-in) is optional. These changes are not deployed by this page. Payment, check-in, and standby flags are visible only to the tournament organizer.

Immediate self-registration uses separate public blakeoutDevSignups entries and a blakeoutDevSignupState revision index. Players can join with a verified account or enter a guest display name on the tournament page. Guest device authentication is separate from your account, never links a name to an account, and grants no lifetime statistics, bracket, or private-roster privileges. There is no approval queue; check-in and team assignment remain separate event logistics.

Each device can self-register one guest per event. The organizer can add more name-only guests on a shared tablet. Guest registration does not sign you out of a verified account.

Casual results use blakeoutDevCasualResults with verified-player blakeoutDevCasualReceipts. Interrupted delivery can be retried without rewriting scores. Larger teams are supported: up to 128 verified participants and 384 per-player game counters, with explicit errors rather than truncation. Self-registration allows up to 128 players per event; tournament brackets still support 32 doubles teams.

auth/operation-not-allowed means the selected sign-in method is not enabled. auth/unauthorized-domain means the hosting domain must be authorized. auth/too-many-requests or a quota error means to wait before retrying; do not repeatedly request emails. permission-denied usually means verification is incomplete, DEV rules have not been published, or this account lacks permission.
